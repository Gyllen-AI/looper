Reading any prior work while building looper.

- Prior work is read adversarially first: evidence of what rotted before it is
  a reference. Reuse is allowed and was taken once — the Rust law is somebody
  else's engine, vendored under a licence that permits it, with its provenance
  written down beside it. Nothing arrives without that file.
- What ports is a decision or a whole component with its own licence. A rule, a
  number or a paragraph does not port: it was measured against other material.
- Borrowing a decision needs the autopsy first, in four lines: what it did, why
  that shape was right or wrong there, what this does instead, what is refused.
  No autopsy, no borrowing.
- A number measured against another project's material is not a measurement of
  ours. Re-measure or do not quote it.
