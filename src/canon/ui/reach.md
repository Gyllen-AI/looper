Being usable by whoever arrives.

- **Every control is reachable without a mouse**, and the order it is reached in
  is the order it is read in.
- **Colour is never the only carrier.** State that is red and green and nothing
  else is invisible to a tenth of the people looking at it.
- **Contrast is a ratio, not a taste.** Say the number the text meets against its
  ground, and check the one that is hardest, not the one that is easiest.
- **A control says what it is to something that cannot see it.** An icon with no
  name is a button nobody can describe, including the person trying to help over
  the phone.
- **Text resizes.** A layout that breaks at larger type is a layout that breaks
  for the people who need it most.
