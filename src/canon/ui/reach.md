Being usable by whoever arrives.

- **Every control is reachable without a mouse**, in the order it is read.
- **Colour is never the only carrier.** Red and green alone is invisible to a
  tenth of the people looking.
- **Contrast is a ratio, not a taste.** Say the number, and check the hardest
  pair, not the easiest.
- **A control says what it is to something that cannot see it.** An icon with no
  name is a button nobody can describe over the phone.
- **Text resizes**, and a layout that breaks at larger type breaks for the people
  who need it most.
