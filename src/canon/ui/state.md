What a screen is allowed to say.

- **Show what the owner of the state answered, never what you hope it will
  answer.** A screen that moves before the answer arrives is lying for as long as
  it takes to be wrong.
- **Loading, empty, and failed are three screens, not one.** An empty list
  because nothing exists and an empty list because the request failed read
  identically and mean opposite things.
- **The client decides nothing it could be told.** Anything computed in two
  places will disagree in one of them, and the one you cannot redeploy is the one
  that stays wrong.
- **A control is not built until somebody has followed it end to end.** A button
  wired to nothing is worse than no button, because it spends trust.
- **Never invent a row to make a screen look alive.** A fabricated one cannot be
  told from a real one the moment anyone reasons from it.
