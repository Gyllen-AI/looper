What a screen is allowed to say.

- **Show what the owner of the state answered, never what you hope it will
  answer.** A screen that moves before the answer arrives is lying until it is
  wrong.
- **Loading, empty, and failed are three screens, not one.**
- **The client decides nothing it could be told.** Anything computed in two
  places disagrees in one, and the one you cannot redeploy stays wrong.
- **A control is not built until somebody has followed it end to end:** the name
  it carries is the name the other side reads, the method it sends is the method
  the route accepts, and what acts on the result is actually running.
- **A panel that cannot load costs a panel, never the page.**
- **Never invent a row to make a screen look alive.**
