Pictures, fonts and the bytes a screen ships.

- **Art is stored at the size it is drawn**, with headroom for a high-density
  screen and no more. A source thirty times its drawn size is memory and download
  spent to look identical.
- **Colour answers one question per system, and the systems never mix.** A hue
  says what a thing is; a tone says whether it needs you. That is the whole
  mechanism by which thirty labels cannot bury one alarm.
- **A design token is defined once and referenced everywhere.** A colour typed
  inline is a colour that will be nearly right somewhere else.
- **Two assets that must stay in step are one asset or are generated.** A sprite
  and its metadata written by hand drift on the first change.
- **An icon set is judged as a set, at true size, on the real ground.** One at a
  time at four times scale is how a set that does not read together ships.
- **Know what you are allowed to ship.** Art extracted from somebody else's
  product is a licensing question before it is a technical one, and the answer is
  quoted rather than assumed.
