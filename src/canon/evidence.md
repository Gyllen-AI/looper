Writing something down that somebody will act on later.

- **Primary sources only, best first: the running system, the artifacts you
  hold, the vendor's own words.** A forum answer is a lead to check, never a
  fact to write down.
- **A measurement carries the number, the date, and what it was measured on**,
  or it is folklore within a month.
- **Absence in a document is not absence in the system.** One measurement on the
  real thing outranks every source that failed to mention it.
- **Say which parts you saw and which you were told**, or nobody can tell which
  half to re-check.
