Writing something down that somebody will act on later.

- **Primary sources only, best first: the running system, the artifacts you
  hold, the vendor's own words.** A blog, a forum answer or a search summary is
  a lead to check, never a fact to write down.
- **A measurement carries the number, the date, and what it was measured on**, or
  it becomes folklore within a month. "Fast", "most", "usually" are opinions
  wearing a number's clothes.
- **Absence in a document is not absence in the system.** One measurement on the
  real thing outranks every secondary source that failed to mention it.
- **Say which parts you saw and which you were told.** A document that mixes the
  two cannot be corrected later, because nobody can tell which half to re-check.
