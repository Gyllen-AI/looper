What a thing costs, decided when it is written.

- **Cost is a property to state, not to discover.** The moment work is written is
  the moment its shape is decided: per request, per row, per frame. Per-row work
  inside a loop over an unbounded set is the incident, and it is invisible at
  the size you tested.
- **A measurement carries the number, the date and the machine.** "Fast" is an
  opinion wearing a number's clothes, and a benchmark on a developer's laptop is
  not a claim about production.
- **An asset is stored at the size it is used.** Art thirty times its drawn size
  is memory and download spent to look identical.
- **Nothing is optimised before it is measured, and nothing large ships without
  being measured once.** Both halves matter: guessing wastes effort, and never
  looking ships the waste.
- **A budget with no enforcement point is a wish.** Say where the number is
  checked, or do not claim it.
