Being able to answer whether it is working.

- **Three answers, never two: working, broken, and could not be asked.** A check
  that cannot reach its subject returns nothing, nothing reads as silence, and
  silence reads as fine.
- **A verdict, not a dump.** Say the state and the number that decided it.
- **Every layer that can fail alone can be asked alone.** One aggregate green
  light cannot say which half is gone.
- **A check that has never failed is protecting something or is dead weight**,
  and only its history tells which. Keep the record.
- **The gap between a thing breaking and somebody knowing is the number that
  matters**, not the number of checks.
