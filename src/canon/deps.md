Taking on code you did not write.

- **Nothing enters unread, unpinned or unowned.** Read it, pin the commit you
  read, name who answers for it when it breaks.
- **Drive it behind your own surfaces**, never as a second process and never as
  its command line.
- **A dependency is a permanent cost:** the updates, the audit, the licence and
  the day it is abandoned, counted before the hours it saves today.
- **The invariant your product rests on is named, and one command proves it
  still holds.**
