Taking on code you did not write.

- **Nothing enters unread, unpinned or unowned.** Read what you are adopting,
  pin it to the commit you read, and name who here answers for it when it breaks.
- **Drive it behind your own surfaces**, never as a second process and never as
  its command line, so its interface changing is a decision you make rather than
  an outage you discover.
- **A dependency is a permanent cost, not a one-off saving.** Count the updates,
  the audit, the licence and the day it is abandoned before counting the hours it
  saves today.
- **The invariant your product rests on is named, and one command proves it still
  holds.** A promise nobody can check is a promise that has already been broken
  somewhere you have not looked.
