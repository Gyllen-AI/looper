Building the part a person looks at: the roof over `ui/state` (what a screen may
say), `ui/assets` (the bytes it ships), `ui/motion` and `ui/reach`. Pull the one
you are in.

- **Judge it at the size it will be used, on real data, by looking at it.** A
  layout reasoned about is not a layout seen.
- **The surface is the contract, in two directions.** What the screen asks for
  and what it is pushed are different shapes, and a screen that polls for what it
  is already sent pays twice.
