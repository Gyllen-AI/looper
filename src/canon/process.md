Finishing a piece of work, and committing it.

- **Done means the checks ran and passed, and the thing was demonstrated
  working.** Not compiled, not written: run.
- **Commit when asked, and never onto the default branch: branch first.** A
  commit message says why, not what.
- **Write the document while the work happens.** The commit gate asks for it.
- **Match the ceremony to the change.** A one-line fix gets a one-line record; a
  boundary moved gets the argument and a branch of its own. The maximum on the
  minimum is how a governed project gets resented.
- **Only a mechanical check counts as a control**, and the gate that carries it
  is named in `doctrine`.
