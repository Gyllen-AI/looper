Reading prior work: an earlier version, another team's repository, a design that
already shipped.

- **Read it adversarially.** It is evidence of what was built and a catalogue of
  what rotted, never a reference to copy from.
- **Borrowing a decision needs the autopsy first, in four lines: what it did,
  why that shape was wrong there, what this does instead, and what it refuses.**
  No autopsy, no borrowing.
- **A thing that exists is not a thing that works.** The question is what it was
  measured doing, not what it was meant to do.
