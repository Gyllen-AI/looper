Chasing a fault you cannot yet see.

- **Add the logging before you chase the fault.** Without it every attempt is a
  guess, and a guess that works teaches nothing.
- **Rare is not fixed.** A fault that got less frequent with no cause named is
  the same fault with worse odds of being caught.
- **Keep the reproduction.** A fault you cannot summon on purpose is one you
  cannot prove gone.
- **One change at a time**, or luck gets written down as a cause.
- **A fix is finished when the failure does not happen under the thing that
  produced it.** Nobody reporting it again means nobody looked.
