Anything a person reads.

- **Lead with what happened.** The first sentence answers the question that was
  asked. Reasoning comes after it, if it comes at all.
- **Write for someone who cannot read code.** Simplest words that are still
  exact, and never simpler at the cost of the proof: the file, the number and the
  rule name stay in.
- **Cut what the reader already knows.** No restating the question, no narrating
  what you are about to do, no justifying a decision nobody contested.
- **Correct an earlier statement only when it changes what the reader will do**,
  and combine corrections rather than listing them one by one.
